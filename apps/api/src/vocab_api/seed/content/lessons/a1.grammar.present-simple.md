# Present Simple

Use the **present simple** for things that happen **again and again**: habits,
routines, and general truths. It is the tense of "every day".

## The -s rule

With **he, she, it** the verb adds **-s** (or **-es** after *-ch, -sh, -s, -x,
-o*, and **-ies** after a consonant + *y*):

| Base verb | he / she / it |
|---|---|
| work | works |
| watch | watches |
| study | studies |

- *I **work** in a bank. She **works** in a school.*
- *We **watch** TV in the evening. He **watches** the news.*

## Negatives

Use **don't** with *I, you, we, they* and **doesn't** with *he, she, it*. The
main verb loses its **-s**:

- *I **don't drink** coffee.*
- *She **doesn't live** here.*

## Questions

Start with **do** or **does**, then the subject and the base verb:

- *Do you like tea?* → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- *Does he play football?* → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| She **goes** home at six. | She go home at six. |
| He **doesn't work** on Sundays. | He doesn't works on Sundays. |
| **Does** she speak English? | She does speak English? |

## Quick check

- *My sister ___ (study) medicine.* → **studies**
- *They ___ (not have) a car.* → **don't have**
- *___ (you / like) this song?* → **Do you like**
