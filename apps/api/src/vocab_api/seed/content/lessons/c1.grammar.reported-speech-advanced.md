# Advanced reported speech

At C1, reporting is about **capturing intent**, not just shifting tenses. Choose
a reporting verb that names what the speaker *did*: promised, suggested,
admitted, insisted.

## Reporting verbs — function over form

| Function | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Command | order, instruct | He **ordered** us to stop. |
| Suggest | suggest, recommend | She **suggested** trying the new API. |
| Promise | promise, agree | They **promised** to ship on time. |
| Admit | admit, concede | He **admitted** the mistake. |
| Insist | insist | She **insisted** on reviewing it. |
| Warn | warn, caution | They **warned** us about the risk. |

## Patterns after reporting verbs

- **+ to-infinitive**: *agreed to help, promised to call, refused to wait.*
- **+ -ing**: *suggested doing, admitted taking, denied seeing.*
- **+ that-clause**: *explained that, mentioned that, stressed that.*
- **+ object + to-infinitive**: *told me to wait, warned us to be careful.*

## The one-step-back rule deepens

With a past reporting verb, shift everything one step:

- `will → would`, `can → could`, `may → might`
- `present → past`, `past → past perfect`
- *"I can't attend." → She said she **couldn't** attend.*

But keep tense when the fact is **still true or timeless**:
*He said the deadline **is** Monday.* (still Monday → present fine)

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| She **suggested trying** it. | She suggested **to try** it. (suggest takes -ing) |
| He **told me to wait**. | He told me wait. (missing to) |
| They **insisted on** reviewing. | They insisted reviewing. (insist on + -ing) |

## Quick check

- *She ___ on checking every field.* → **insisted**
- *He ___ to help with the migration.* → **agreed**
- *They warned us ___ the outage.* → **about**
- *She said she ___ finish by noon.* → **would**

Name the function with the verb, then let the pattern follow. The reporting verb is the skeleton; the shift is the detail.
