# Present perfect: have done vs did

Present perfect connects **the past to now**. Past simple keeps them separate.
That single idea decides almost every choice between the two.

## When to use present perfect (`have/has + V3`)

1. **Result visible now**: `I've lost my key.` (→ I can't get in right now)
2. **Life experience up to now**: `Have you ever been to Japan?`
3. **Unfinished time period**: `I've written two reports this week.` (the week is not over)

## When to use past simple (`V2`)

The action is **finished and its time is named or closed**:

- `I lost my key yesterday.`
- `She worked in London in 2019.`
- `I was in Japan last year.`

Time words that close the door: `yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago, when I was a student`.

Time words that keep it open: `just, already, yet, ever, never, recently, this week, so far`.

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| I've **already** finished. | I finished **already**. (word order) |
| Did you **see** it yesterday? | ~~Have you seen~~ it yesterday? (closed time → past simple) |
| I **have been** to London. | I **have went** to London. (gone with have → been for experience) |

## Experience vs single past event

- `I've been to the office.` — at some point in my life, here I am / now I know it.
- `I went to the office at 9.` — one finished trip, time stated.

## Quick check

- *___ you ever ___ sushi?* → **Have … eaten**
- *I ___ the report an hour ago.* → **sent**
- *She's ___ left.* → **just**

Ask: *is the time window closed?* Closed → past simple. Still open / result now → present perfect.
