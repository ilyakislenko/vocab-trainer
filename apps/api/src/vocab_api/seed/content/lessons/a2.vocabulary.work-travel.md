# Work & travel

Two of the most common topics in small talk are **work** and **travel**. This
lesson gives you the words to handle both without searching your memory.

## Jobs

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| engineer | builds and fixes technical systems |
| nurse | cares for people in a hospital |
| accountant | manages money and taxes |
| salesperson | sells products |
| receptionist | greets guests, answers calls |
| mechanic | repairs cars and machines |

Ask: *What do you do?* → *I work as a nurse.*

## Work situations

- **a full-time / part-time job**
- **work from home** — do your job from home
- **a day off** — a day you do not work
- **on holiday** — away from work, travelling

## Travel

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| flight | a trip by plane |
| boarding pass | ticket to get on the plane |
| luggage | your bags and suitcases |
| platform | the place where the train stops |
| single / return ticket | one way / there and back |
| book | reserve in advance |

- *I booked a **return ticket** to Prague.*
- *The **flight** leaves from terminal two.*

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| I work **as** a mechanic. | I work like a mechanic. |
| a **return** ticket | a returnable ticket |
| I'm **on holiday**. | I'm at holiday. |

## Quick check

- *She works ___ a receptionist.* → **as**
- *My ___ leaves at 9 a.m.* (plane trip) → **flight**
- *I need a ___ ticket — just one way.* → **single**
