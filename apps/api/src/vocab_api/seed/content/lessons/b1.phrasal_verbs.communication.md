# Phrasal verbs: communication

Communication phrasal verbs help you describe **speaking, writing, and listening**
with precision. One verb can have several particles — each a different meaning.

## Speaking & messages

| Phrasal verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| speak **up** | talk louder / defend | Please speak up on the call. |
| bring **up** | raise a topic | She brought up the budget. |
| point **out** | draw attention to | He pointed out a risk. |
| spell **out** | explain clearly | Let me spell out the terms. |
| get **across** | communicate successfully | I didn't get my idea across. |
| hear **from** | receive news from | We heard from the client. |
| fill **in** | inform someone | Fill me in on the details. |
| keep in **touch** | stay in contact | Let's keep in touch. |

## One verb, many meanings

**call**: *call back* (return a call), *call off* (cancel), *call on* (visit/ask),
*call up* (phone).

**turn**: *turn down* (reject), *turn up* (arrive), *turn into* (become),
*turn out* (end up).

- *They called **off** the demo.* (cancelled)
- *She turned **down** the offer.* (rejected)
- *He turned **up** late.* (arrived)

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| Can you **call me back**? | Can you call back me? (pronoun middle) |
| She **brought up** the topic. | She brought the topic up — also OK (separable). |
| The meeting was **called off**. | The meeting was cancelled off. |

## Strategy

Group by particle and study the meaning families. Then place pronouns correctly:
*point it out, fill me in, hear from him* (inseparable: *hear from him*, not
"hear him from").

## Quick check

- *The webinar was ___ off at the last minute.* → **called**
- *Please ___ me in on the plan.* → **fill**
- *He ___ down the promotion.* → **turned**
- *It ___ out that she was right.* → **turned**

The particle is the meaning switch. Master the particles and you unlock dozens of verbs at once.
