# Reported speech: backshift

When you report what someone said, you usually move the verb **one step back in
time**. This is called *backshift*.

## The shift

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I **am** busy." | She said she **was** busy. |
| "I **work** here." | He said he **worked** there. |
| "I **will** call." | He said he **would** call. |
| "I **have done** it." | She said she **had done** it. |

Not only verbs shift — so do time and place words:

- `today → that day`, `yesterday → the day before`, `here → there`, `this → that`.

## Reporting questions

- **Yes/no** → `if/whether` + statement order: *"Are you ready?" → He asked if I was ready.*
- **Wh-questions** → keep the question word + statement order: *"Where do you work?"
  → He asked where I worked.*

Question order is **statement order** after the reporting verb — no `do/did`:
*She asked where I worked.* (not "where did I work").

## Reporting requests

Use `ask/tell + person + to + infinitive`:

- *"Please help me." → He asked me to help him.*
- *"Don't be late." → She told me not to be late.*

## When NOT to backshift

If the situation is **still true** or the report is immediate, present stays:

- *"I work at Elingo."* said yesterday, still true → *He said he works at Elingo.*
  (both `works` and `worked` are correct; `works` keeps it relevant now)

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| He said he **had** seen it. | He said he **has** seen it. (backshift) |
| She asked **if** I was ready. | She asked that I was ready. |
| He told me **to** wait. | He told me wait. (missing to) |

## Quick check

- *"I'm tired"* → *He said he ___ tired.* → **was**
- *"Are you free?"* → *She asked ___ I was free.* → **if**
- *"Please don't shout."* → *He asked me ___ to shout.* → **not**

Look at the reporting verb's tense. Past reporting verb → shift one step back. Still-true facts may stay.
