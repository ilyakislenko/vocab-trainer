# Present Continuous

Use the **present continuous** — **am/is/are + verb-ing** — for something
**happening right now** and for **arrangements** you have already made.

## Form

| Subject | be + -ing |
|---|---|
| I | am working |
| he / she / it | is working |
| you / we / they | are working |

Spelling: *work → working, make → making* (drop *e*), *run → running* (double
the final consonant).

## Actions in progress now

- *I **am writing** an email at the moment.*
- *They **are watching** a film right now.*

## Future arrangements

With a time expression, the same form expresses a **fixed plan**:

- *We **are meeting** the client tomorrow at ten.*
- *She **is flying** to Berlin next week.*

## Present simple vs continuous

- **Present simple** — habits and facts: *I **work** in a bank.*
- **Present continuous** — in progress or arranged: *I **am working** late
  tonight.*

## Common mistakes

| Correct | Common mistake |
|---|---|
| She **is sleeping** now. | She sleeps now. |
| They **are playing** football. | They is playing football. |
| I **am making** dinner. | I am make dinner. |

## Quick check

- *Listen! Someone ___ (sing).* → **is singing**
- *We ___ (have) a meeting on Monday.* → **are having**
- *He ___ (not / work) today.* → **isn't working**
